Each answer is yes, no or cannot tell, and "cannot tell" never counts as yes. Tap a question to see the evaluator's checks behind it.

Who stands behind the claim

Arrows point to referenced credentials: authority through termsOfUse, support through evidence, each bound to exact bytes by relatedResource. Badges show whether each signature verified. Tap a box to read that credential.

Exact signed bytes

  


  

    
Credentials represent authority. They do not create it.

    
Every answer comes from the repository's own evaluators (evaluateRmSlice, evaluateCalSlice, evaluateGsSlice for GS certificates and product passports), bundled for the browser: pinned contexts and schemas, issuer keys from controller documents, Ed25519 signatures over the JSON-LD safe-mode canonical form, exact-byte digests, validity and Bitstring status lists, then claim mapping, authority routes and scope, required support and any decision rule. Nothing is fetched. The question is evaluated as of 25 September 2026, the fixtures' reference time, because status lists expire; a real verifier would use the current time and fresh status.
