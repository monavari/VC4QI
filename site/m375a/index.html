The seven gates a verifier checks

    
The verifier picks the profile; the credential cannot pick a weaker one. Each answer is yes, no or cannot tell, and cannot tell never counts as yes. A gate only reads facts from credentials that passed the gates before it.

    The signed credentials are the repository's RM v1 test fixtures (bindings/experimental/rm-v1), signed with eddsa-rdfc-2022. Gates 0 to 3 run the repository's verifyRmArtifact, bundled for the browser: pinned contexts and schemas, the issuer's own key from its controller document, the Ed25519 signature over the JSON-LD safe-mode canonical form, exact-byte digests and validity. Gates 4 to 6 preview the authority, scope, support and decision rules that the repository is still implementing.
