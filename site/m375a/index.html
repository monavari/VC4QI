Credentials represent authority. They do not create it.

    
All seven gates run the repository's evaluateRmSlice, bundled for the browser: pinned contexts and schemas, issuer keys from controller documents, Ed25519 signatures over the JSON-LD safe-mode canonical form, exact-byte digests, validity and Bitstring status lists, then claim mapping, authority routes and scope, the required study and the verifier's decision rule. The question is evaluated as of 25 September 2026, the fixtures' reference time, because status lists expire; a real verifier would use the current time and fresh status.
