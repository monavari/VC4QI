# Experimental calibration (DCC) v1 binding

A standards-first binding for the **calibration-direct-accreditation**,
**calibration-capability** and **nmi-legal-mandate** use cases, migrated from the legacy
examples in `testdata/examples/`. It
reuses the shared gate 0–3 pipeline (`reliance/binding-chain.ts`) and adds calibration
gates 4–6 (`reliance/cal-v1-evaluator.ts`, `cal-v1-slice.ts`; Python
`qi_vc_core/reliance/cal_v1.py`).

- `manifest.json`, `catalog.json`: the binding and its pinned resources.
- `resources/contexts/cal-1.jsonld`: hand-authored context.
- `resources/schemas/`: generated by `scripts/cal-v1/build-resources.mjs` (`--check`).
- `profiles/`: fictional verifier profiles, each stating `bindingRules.applyCmcFloor`:
  `cal-verifier-1.json` (direct accreditation), `cal-verifier-capability-1.json`
  (operational scope) and `cal-verifier-nmi-1.json` (statutory mandate).
- `test-vectors/signed/`: generated by
  `packages/core-ts/scripts/generate-cal-v1-artifacts.ts` (`--check`). It contains
  controller documents for the fictional NAB and laboratory (the same insecure seeds as
  RM v1) and their status lists. It also has `CAL-A`, an accreditation for pressure
  calibration 0–10 MPa by PressureComparison with a CMC of 0.5 kPa, which also permits
  maintaining a calibration scope, and `DCC-1`, a certificate with two measurement
  groups. `CAL-O` is the laboratory's own operational scope (0–2 MPa, CMC 0.8 kPa)
  within `CAL-A`, and `DCC-2` is issued under it. `CAL-M` is a fictional ministry's
  statutory mandate to a national metrology institute (0–100 MPa by PressureBalance,
  CMC 0.2 kPa), and `DCC-N` is issued under it.

Each selected measurement group is one required claim. One complete scope record must
cover the group's quantity kind, every method it names and every result. When the
profile applies it, an expanded uncertainty below the CMC floor contradicts scope. The
binding carries a JSON-LD simplification of DCC results, not native DCC XML.

An operational scope must lie within one accreditation record (quantity kind, methods,
range and, when the floor applies, a CMC not below the accreditation's), and claims are
covered by the operational scope only. The statutory route needs no accreditation root.
Keys, parties and grants are fictional and have no legal effect.
