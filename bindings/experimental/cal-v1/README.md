# Experimental calibration (DCC) v1 binding

A standards-first binding for the **calibration-direct-accreditation**,
**calibration-capability**, **nmi-legal-mandate** and **test-report-supported-dcc** use
cases, migrated from the v0.3 examples. It reuses the shared gate 0–3 pipeline (`reliance/binding-chain.ts`) and adds calibration
gates 4–6 (`reliance/cal-v1-evaluator.ts`, `cal-v1-slice.ts`; Python
`qi_vc_core/reliance/cal_v1.py`).

- `manifest.json`, `catalog.json`: the binding and its pinned resources.
- `resources/contexts/cal-1.jsonld`: hand-authored context.
- `resources/schemas/`: generated by `scripts/cal-v1/build-resources.mjs` (`--check`).
- `profiles/`: fictional verifier profiles, each stating `bindingRules.applyCmcFloor`:
  `cal-verifier-1.json` (direct accreditation), `cal-verifier-capability-1.json`
  (operational scope), `cal-verifier-nmi-1.json` (statutory mandate) and
  `cal-verifier-test-report-1.json` (test report with instrument-calibration support).
  `cal-verifier-state-1.json` and `cal-verifier-global-aci-1.json` trust the state or
  Global ACI instead of the accreditation body, for direct accreditation and
  operational scopes.
- `test-vectors/signed/`: generated by
  `packages/core-ts/scripts/generate-cal-v1-artifacts.ts` (`--check`). It contains
  controller documents for the fictional NAB and laboratory (the same insecure seeds as
  RM v1) and their status lists. It also has `CAL-A`, an accreditation for pressure
  calibration 0–10 MPa by PressureComparison with a CMC of 0.5 kPa, which also permits
  maintaining a calibration scope, and `DCC-1`, a certificate with two measurement
  groups. `CAL-O` is the laboratory's own operational scope (0–2 MPa, CMC 0.8 kPa)
  within `CAL-A`, and `DCC-2` is issued under it. `CAL-M` is a fictional ministry's
  statutory mandate to a national metrology institute (0–100 MPa by PressureBalance,
  CMC 0.2 kPa), and `DCC-N` is issued under it. `CAL-T` is a testing accreditation of a
  fictional testing laboratory, and `REPORT-1` is its test report, which cites `DCC-1` as
  the calibration of the pressure transmitter it used.

The accreditation body is the RM binding's NAB, with the same two roots. `CAL-A` and
`CAL-T` name the state's appointment `APP-NAB` (`IssuedUnderPolicy`) and Global ACI's
recognition `REC-NAB` (`RecognitionPolicy`, mirrored as `issuer.recognizedIn`), each
with its digest, as the RM accreditations do. The roots are signed once, by the RM
generator: the signed catalog lists them, and the state's and Global ACI's controllers
and status lists, where they are in `../rm-v1/test-vectors/signed/`, and `catalog.json`
installs the RM carriers needed to read them (the RM v1 and Recognized Entities stand-in
contexts, and RM's appointment, recognition and status-list schemas). A verifier that
anchors the accreditation body never relies on them. One that anchors the state accepts
the body through `APP-NAB`, which permits `CalAccreditation` in Germany; one that anchors
Global ACI accepts it through `REC-NAB`, for Calibration (certificates) or Testing (test
reports) under ISO/IEC 17025:2017. The deciding root joins the route's witnesses.

Each selected measurement group is one required claim. One complete scope record must
cover the group's quantity kind, every method it names and every result. When the
profile applies it, an expanded uncertainty below the CMC floor contradicts scope. The
binding carries a JSON-LD simplification of DCC results, not native DCC XML: as in the
DCC, each result is a PTB D-SI 2.2.1 real (`results[]/real`, with QUDT quantity kind
`Pressure`, a D-SI unit `\pascal`, `\kilo\pascal` or `\mega\pascal`, and an `expandedMU`),
and every result of a group names the same quantity kind, which is the group's. Scope
ranges and CMC floors use the same D-SI units; see
[bindings](../../../docs/bindings.md#quantity-values-ptb-d-si).

An operational scope must lie within one accreditation record (quantity kind, methods,
range and, when the floor applies, a CMC not below the accreditation's), and claims are
covered by the operational scope only. The statutory route needs no accreditation root. A test report requires its
instrument's calibration as support: the same instrument and quantity, calibrated before
the test and valid at it, and independently authorized for every one of its groups.
Keys, parties and grants are fictional and have no legal effect.
