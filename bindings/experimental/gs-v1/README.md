# Experimental GS certification v1 binding

A standards-first binding for the **gs-scheme-authorization** use case, migrated from
the v0.3 example of the same name. It reuses the shared
gate 0–3 pipeline (`reliance/binding-chain.ts`) and adds GS gates 4–6
(`reliance/gs-v1-evaluator.ts`, `gs-v1-slice.ts`; Python
`qi_vc_core/reliance/gs_v1.py`).

- `manifest.json`, `catalog.json`: the binding and its pinned resources.
- `resources/contexts/gs-1.jsonld`: hand-authored context.
- `resources/schemas/`: generated by `scripts/gs-v1/build-resources.mjs` (`--check`).
- `profiles/gs-verifier-mark-1.json` and `profiles/gs-verifier-dpp-1.json`: fictional
  verifier profiles for a GS-mark credential (route `gs-certified-product`) and for a
  product passport (route `responsible-economic-operator`), described below.
- `profiles/gs-verifier-1.json`: fictional verifier profile with one route,
  `competence-and-scheme-permission`. Its anchors are the NAB, a fictional accreditation
  body in the role DAkkS plays in Germany (accrediting certification bodies and testing
  laboratories), and a fictional authority in the ZLS role (authorizing GS
  certification). Real institutions are not named in signed fixtures.
- `test-vectors/signed/`: generated by
  `packages/core-ts/scripts/generate-gs-v1-artifacts.ts` (`--check`), shaped like a GS
  certification chain (GS mark → GS certificate → accreditation, scheme authorization and
  studies):
  - controller documents and status lists for the NAB, the ZLS-role authority, the GS
    body, an external testing laboratory, the manufacturer and another company;
  - `GS-A`, the NAB's accreditation of the GS body for certifying (which includes factory
    inspection) and testing toys (EN 71-1, EN 71-2) and household appliances
    (EN 60335-1, EN 60335-2-23), and `TL-A`, the NAB's accreditation of the external
    laboratory for testing;
  - `GS-S`, the ZLS-role authorization to award the GS mark, for household appliances only;
  - studies: `FI-1`, the factory inspection of the manufacturer; `TR-1` and `TR-3`,
    in-house type examinations (hair dryer HD-01, toy); `TR-2`, the external laboratory's
    type examination of hair dryer HD-02;
  - `GSC-1` (HD-01), `GSC-3` (HD-02, external laboratory) and `GSC-2` (a toy: GS-A covers
    it, GS-S does not), each citing its type examination and `FI-1` as `evidence`;
  - `GSM-1` to `GSM-3`, experimental GS-mark credentials: the manufacturer's claim that one
    unit carries the GS mark, through GSC-1 to GSC-3; `GSM-4`, a unit placed on the market
    before GSC-1 was issued; `GSM-5`, issued by another company citing GSC-1; and
    `GSM-6`, an HD-02 unit whose marking claims the mark for HD-01;
  - `DPP-1` to `DPP-3`, experimental product passports: `DPP-1` for unit sn-0042, citing
    its `GSM-1`; `DPP-2` for the early unit sn-0001, citing `GSM-4`; and `DPP-3` for an
    HD-02 unit sn-0050 that cites `GSM-1`, the credential of another unit.

The GS mark is relied on only through the complete route: competence AND scheme
permission. Each half needs its own typed reference, grantee, activity, anchor purpose
and validity at the certification time. The certified category and standards must be
covered by one competence record, and the category by one scheme record. Neither
incomplete basis alone establishes the route. The certificate also needs its two
studies (gate 6): a type examination of the model that covers its category and every
certified standard, and a factory inspection of its manufacturer. Each must have passed,
precede the certification, and be issued under an anchored accreditation of its issuer
that permits the activity and was in force at the study: testing for a type
examination, and certification for a factory inspection, which is part of the
certification body's certification competence rather than a separately accredited
activity. This is a fictional profile example, not a universal GS or legal rule. Keys,
parties and grants are fictional and have no legal effect.

**Experimental GS-mark credential.** A `GsMarkCredential` is the manufacturer's claim that
one serial-numbered unit carries the GS mark. Under `gs-verifier-mark-1` the claim
(`/credentialSubject/marking`) is authorized only through the route `gs-certified-product`.
It must name the credential's own model (otherwise it is contradicted), and it needs a
typed reference to a GS certificate that names the issuer as manufacturer, certifies that
model, was in force when the unit was placed on the market, and itself holds competence AND
scheme permission with its certification covered. Today's GS scheme has no per-unit
credential; the GS body issues the certificate and the manufacturer applies the mark.

**Experimental product passport.** A `ProductPassport` is the unit's passport, shaped after
the EU Digital Product Passport of the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation
(ESPR) and its list of passport information: the unique product identifier (the subject),
product name and model, GTIN, commodity code, the responsible economic operator and the
manufacturing facility, when the unit was placed on the market, technical data (rated
power), sustainability data (spare-part availability) and compliance information. The
compliance information lists the GS mark (`/credentialSubject/complianceInformation/gsMark`)
and cites the unit's GS-mark credential as `evidence`, bound by digest.

Under `gs-verifier-dpp-1` the passport is relied on as the declaration of its responsible
economic operator (route `responsible-economic-operator`: the issuer must be the operator
the passport names; no grant credential exists, so this is the profile's rule). Its
GS-mark entry is *supported* by the cited GS-mark credential (gate 6), which keeps its own
authority route. That credential must be usable, for the same unit, issued by the same
operator, claim the mark for the passport's model and itself hold `gs-certified-product`
with the mark covered. This is the paper's "aggregated results" shape: the container
relies on each included result as support, and each result retains its own authority
route. The binding interprets only the GS-mark entry; selecting any other passport
section is `not_established` ("no interpretation in this binding"), so a request is never
accepted for passport content the verifier cannot evaluate.

This is a research illustration, not ESPR conformance: no delegated act defines a passport
for this product group here, the section names are illustrative, and the GTIN, commodity
code and identifiers are fictional. That the physical unit really is this model remains
the manufacturer's claim, which no credential check can establish.

## Expected outcomes

Every credential below is correctly signed; the decisions follow from the profile.

| Target | Profile | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GSC-1` (hair dryer HD-01, in-house type examination) | `gs-verifier-1` | accept | Competence and scheme permission both cover household appliances; TR-1 and FI-1 hold |
| `GSC-3` (hair dryer HD-02, external laboratory) | `gs-verifier-1` | accept | As GSC-1; TR-2 is authorized by the laboratory's own accreditation TL-A |
| `GSC-2` (toy) | `gs-verifier-1` | reject | GS-A covers toys, but GS-S does not permit awarding the mark for toys: competence without scheme permission |
| `GSM-1`, `GSM-3` | `gs-verifier-mark-1` | accept | The certificate names the manufacturer, certifies the model, was in force when the unit was placed on the market and holds its own route |
| `GSM-2` | `gs-verifier-mark-1` | reject | Its certificate GSC-2 is not covered by the scheme permission |
| `GSM-4` | `gs-verifier-mark-1` | not accepted | The unit was placed on the market (15 Feb 2026) before GSC-1 was valid (1 Mar 2026) |
| `GSM-5` | `gs-verifier-mark-1` | reject | Issued by another company; the certificate's manufacturer is not the issuer |
| `GSM-6` | `gs-verifier-mark-1` | reject | An HD-02 unit whose marking claims the mark for HD-01: contradicted at mapping |
| `DPP-1` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | accept | Issued by its economic operator; its GS-mark entry is supported by GSM-1, which holds its own route |
| `DPP-2` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | not established | Its GSM-4 cannot be authorized by a certificate issued after the unit was placed on the market |
| `DPP-3` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | reject | It cites GSM-1, the credential of another unit and model |
| `DPP-1`, GS-mark entry and sustainability section selected | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | not established | The GS-mark entry is established; the sustainability section has no interpretation in this binding |

A withheld type examination, factory inspection, certificate or GS-mark credential leaves the result
`not_established`, not rejected.

Documented simplifications: only the initial factory inspection is checked, not
ongoing surveillance; the scheme permission is checked at the certification time, so
its later expiry (GS-S ends on 1 Jan 2027, the certificates run to 2031) is not
modelled; and the toy restriction is fictional, chosen to separate competence from
scheme permission.
