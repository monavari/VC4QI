# Experimental GS certification v1 binding

A standards-first binding for the **gs-scheme-authorization** use case, migrated from
the legacy example in `testdata/examples/gs-scheme-authorization`. It reuses the shared
gate 0–3 pipeline (`reliance/binding-chain.ts`) and adds GS gates 4–6
(`reliance/gs-v1-evaluator.ts`, `gs-v1-slice.ts`; Python
`qi_vc_core/reliance/gs_v1.py`).

- `manifest.json`, `catalog.json`: the binding and its pinned resources.
- `resources/contexts/gs-1.jsonld`: hand-authored context.
- `resources/schemas/`: generated by `scripts/gs-v1/build-resources.mjs` (`--check`).
- `profiles/gs-verifier-1.json`: fictional verifier profile with one route,
  `competence-and-scheme-permission`. It has two anchors: the NAB for accrediting
  certification bodies, and a fictional scheme owner for authorizing GS certification.
- `test-vectors/signed/`: generated by
  `packages/core-ts/scripts/generate-gs-v1-artifacts.ts` (`--check`):
  - controller documents and status lists for the NAB, the scheme owner and the GS body;
  - `GS-A`, the body's accreditation (competence) for toys (EN 71-1, EN 71-2) and
    household appliances (EN 60335-1, EN 60335-2-23);
  - `GS-S`, the scheme owner's authorization to award the GS mark, for toys only;
  - `GSC-1`, a GS certificate for a toy against EN 71-1 that cites both.

The GS mark is relied on only through the complete route: competence AND scheme
permission. Each half needs its own typed reference, grantee, activity, anchor purpose
and validity at the certification time. The certified category and standards must be
covered by one competence record, and the category by one scheme record. Neither
incomplete basis alone establishes the route. This is a fictional profile example, not a
universal GS or legal rule. Keys, parties and grants are fictional and have no legal
effect.

The two GS application variants (`gs-hair-dryer-hitl` and
`gs-hair-dryer-external-test-lab-hitl`) are retained unchanged as signed legacy
fixtures. They are evaluated under the explicit legacy profile, and their application
assessments are not migrated to this binding.
