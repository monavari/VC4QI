# Experimental GS certification v1 binding

A standards-first binding for the **gs-scheme-authorization** use case, migrated from
the v0.3 example of the same name. It reuses the shared
gate 0–3 pipeline (`reliance/binding-chain.ts`) and adds GS gates 4–6
(`reliance/gs-v1-evaluator.ts`, `gs-v1-slice.ts`; Python
`qi_vc_core/reliance/gs_v1.py`).

- `manifest.json`, `catalog.json`: the binding and its pinned resources.
- `resources/contexts/gs-1.jsonld`: hand-authored context.
- `resources/schemas/`: generated by `scripts/gs-v1/build-resources.mjs` (`--check`).
- `profiles/gs-verifier-1.json`: fictional verifier profile with one route,
  `competence-and-scheme-permission`. Its anchors are the NAB, a fictional accreditation
  body in the role DAkkS plays in Germany (accrediting certification bodies and testing
  laboratories), and a fictional authority in the ZLS role (authorizing GS
  certification). Real institutions are not named in signed fixtures.
- `test-vectors/signed/`: generated by
  `packages/core-ts/scripts/generate-gs-v1-artifacts.ts` (`--check`), shaped like a GS
  certification chain (GS mark → GS certificate → accreditation, scheme authorization and
  studies):
  - controller documents and status lists for the NAB, the ZLS-role authority, the GS
    body, an external testing laboratory, the manufacturer and another company;
  - `GS-A`, the NAB's accreditation of the GS body for certifying (which includes factory
    inspection) and testing toys (EN 71-1, EN 71-2) and household appliances
    (EN 60335-1, EN 60335-2-23), and `TL-A`, the NAB's accreditation of the external
    laboratory for testing;
  - `GS-S`, the ZLS-role authorization to award the GS mark, for household appliances only;
  - studies: `FI-1`, the factory inspection of the manufacturer; `TR-1` and `TR-3`,
    in-house type examinations (hair dryer HD-01, toy); `TR-2`, the external laboratory's
    type examination of hair dryer HD-02;
  - `GSC-1` (HD-01), `GSC-3` (HD-02, external laboratory) and `GSC-2` (a toy: GS-A covers
    it, GS-S does not), each citing its type examination and `FI-1` as `evidence`;
  - `DPP-1` to `DPP-3`, experimental manufacturer product credentials claiming the GS
    mark for one unit
    through GSC-1 to GSC-3; `DPP-4`, a unit placed on the market before GSC-1 was issued;
    and `DPP-5`, issued by another company citing GSC-1.

The GS mark is relied on only through the complete route: competence AND scheme
permission. Each half needs its own typed reference, grantee, activity, anchor purpose
and validity at the certification time. The certified category and standards must be
covered by one competence record, and the category by one scheme record. Neither
incomplete basis alone establishes the route. The certificate also needs its two
studies (gate 6): a type examination of the model that covers its category and every
certified standard, and a factory inspection of its manufacturer. Each must have passed,
precede the certification, and be issued under an anchored accreditation of its issuer
that permits the activity and was in force at the study: testing for a type
examination, and certification for a factory inspection, which is part of the
certification body's certification competence rather than a separately accredited
activity. This is a fictional profile example, not a universal GS or legal rule. Keys, parties and grants are fictional and have no legal
effect.

**Experimental product passport.** Under `gs-verifier-dpp-1`, a passport's GS-mark claim
(`/credentialSubject/marking`) is authorized only through the route `gs-certified-product`:
a typed reference to a GS certificate that names the passport issuer as manufacturer,
certifies the passport's model, was in force when the unit was placed on the market, and
itself holds competence AND scheme permission with its certification covered. It is a
research illustration of reliance on a product passport, not EU Digital Product Passport
(ESPR) conformance. Today's GS scheme has no per-unit credential: the GS body issues the
certificate and the manufacturer applies the mark. `DPP-1` to `DPP-5` model a
manufacturer's digital claim that one serial-numbered unit is covered by a certificate.

## Expected outcomes

Every credential below is correctly signed; the decisions follow from the profile.

| Target | Profile | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GSC-1` (hair dryer HD-01, in-house type examination) | `gs-verifier-1` | accept | Competence and scheme permission both cover household appliances; TR-1 and FI-1 hold |
| `GSC-3` (hair dryer HD-02, external laboratory) | `gs-verifier-1` | accept | As GSC-1; TR-2 is authorized by the laboratory's own accreditation TL-A |
| `GSC-2` (toy) | `gs-verifier-1` | reject | GS-A covers toys, but GS-S does not permit awarding the mark for toys: competence without scheme permission |
| `DPP-1`, `DPP-3` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | accept | The certificate names the manufacturer, certifies the model, was in force when the unit was placed on the market and holds its own route |
| `DPP-2` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | reject | Its certificate GSC-2 is not covered by the scheme permission |
| `DPP-4` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | not accepted | The unit was placed on the market (15 Feb 2026) before GSC-1 was valid (1 Mar 2026) |
| `DPP-5` | `gs-verifier-dpp-1` | reject | Issued by another company; the certificate's manufacturer is not the passport issuer |

A withheld type examination, factory inspection or certificate leaves the result
`not_established`, not rejected.

Documented simplifications: only the initial factory inspection is checked, not
ongoing surveillance; the scheme permission is checked at the certification time, so
its later expiry (GS-S ends on 1 Jan 2027, the certificates run to 2031) is not
modelled; and the toy restriction is fictional, chosen to separate competence from
scheme permission.
